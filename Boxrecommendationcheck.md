
---- Essential terms to keep in mind----

Dimensions: Ensure the cumulative volume of the items (multiplied by the quantity) does not exceed the box's inner volume multiplied by 0.90 (the 90% efficiency buffer).

Weight: Ensure total weight is less than or equal to box.max_weight.

3D Fit: Ensure the individual item dimensions can be rotated and placed inside the box dimensions without violating length, width, or height limits.
